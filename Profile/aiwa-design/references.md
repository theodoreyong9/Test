# References for the site (home, product, research)

Concept, in one sentence: a page that behaves like a thread of light pulled through the page by the scroll, with a living face inside the headline.
Signature element: the thread (an SVG line drawn by the scroll from the orb to the last call, with a bright point leading it), and the face inside the first sentence.

Research made with `skills/kit/design-research` (brief: manifesto site about an AI that carries you to people, scroll-driven storytelling, kinetic typography, luminous generative visuals, dark; format site). Five references came back, of which two were really looked at; the other two (revelatio.studio, hobro.digital) only showed a loading screen in their captures and are not cited.

## Kept

### Paul Kalkbrenner (paulkalkbrenner.net)
- Address: https://www.paulkalkbrenner.net/ , catalogue id `paulkalkbrenner.net`
- Captures looked at: screenshots/paulkalkbrenner.net-desktop-desktop.jpg
- Measured: oversized sans type, kinetic type, scroll-driven motion, reveal on scroll, dark palette (largest text 150 to 230 px across the five references).
- Seen on the capture, which the measures do not say: the name is the whole page, and a small living picture sits inside the word, in a gap of the type, instead of beside it.
- Taken: a living picture inside the headline. Here: the face of Aiwa, a canvas in a rounded gap of the first sentence ("you to [face] people").

### Dominik Kostolnik (dkton.at)
- Address: https://dkton.at/ , catalogue id `dkton.at`
- Captures looked at: screenshots/dkton.at-desktop-desktop.jpg
- Measured: oversized kinetic type, scroll-driven motion, dark palette, no technical constraint for a site.
- Seen on the capture: soft rounded forms layered behind a massive weight of type, so the type and the shapes read as one scene.
- Taken: rounded, layered forms behind big type (here the glowing blobs and the figures behind the titles of each scene) and the end of square boxes in favour of pills.

## What the references share and was applied
Oversized and kinetic type (here: the title leans with the speed of the scroll), fullscreen sections that the scroll plays (here: the scenes), motion driven by the scroll, a dark restrained palette. Not copied: the identity, the layout, the content or the assets of either site.
